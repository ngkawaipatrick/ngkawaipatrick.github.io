Ch 1 - Physics, DNA, cell cycle

Numbers

  • Ionisation in water ~33 eV; C=C bond 4.9 eV. 1 MeV e- → ~30,000 ion pairs.
  • Lethal 4 Gy x 70 kg = 280 J = 67 cal → +0.001 °C. Kills by DNA damage, not heat.
  • λ(Å) = 12.4 / E(keV)
  • DNA helix 2 nm wide; A-T 2 H-bonds, G-C 3; purines A,G ("Pure As Gold"); phosphate on 5' carbon, links to 3' of next. Base on 1'.
  • Indirect action ~2/3 of X-ray damage; radicals must form within 4 nm of DNA. Cells 70-80% water → water = main radiolysis target.

Photon interactions (3)

Water crossovers: PE = Compton at ~30 keV; Compton = pair at ~24 MeV.

Timescale (rank: fast → slow)

10-15 s ionisation → 10-10 ion radicals → 10-9 free radicals (OH•) → 10-5 DNA radical fixed by O₂ or restored by -SH → 10-3 chemistry over → hours-months cell death / early-late effects → years cancer → generations heritable.

Cell cycle

  • G1 → S → G2 → M; G0 = resting (neurons for life). Interphase = G1+S+G2.
  • G1 varies most → sets doubling time; S, G2, M ~constant (~10 h together). Typical cancer line CDT ~24 h.
  • Mitosis P-M-A-T; metaphase = line up on equatorial plate.
  • Cyclin-CDK pairs (4): D-CDK4/6 (early/mid G1); E-CDK2 (G1/S); A-CDK2 (S/G2); B-CDK1 (G2/M). CDK2 = G1→S and S/G2.
  • Checkpoints: G1/S "safe to copy?"; G2/M "copied correctly?".
  • Telomerase → immortal cancer cells; 2009 Nobel. Watson-Crick 1953, Nobel 1962, Photo 51 (Franklin).
  • History: Rontgen 1895; Becquerel 1896; Curies 1898 (Po, Ra).

1.1-1.3, 1.6 basics

  • Levels of effect (4): molecule (radicals, DNA/protein) → cell (aberration, mutation, death, immortalisation) → tissue (cancer, necrosis, organ failure) → whole body (death). "Game theory - it's gambling": all events probabilistic.
  • Misconceptions: microwave, mobile RF = non-ionising; Fukushima food monitored; chest X-ray ~0.1 mGy† → 0.004 DSB/cell = 1 cell in 250, repaired.
  • Sources: natural (cosmic, radon, rocks, food/body); artificial ~15% of exposure, >90% of that medical. Protection (4): time, decay, distance (1/r²), shielding.
  • X-ray = outside nucleus (bremsstrahlung/shells); γ = inside nucleus; same E behaves the same. 1 eV = 1.6x10-19 J. Particles: e-, e+, p (1840 me), α (2p2n), n, heavy ions (C).
  • Directly ionising = charged (e, p, α, C); indirectly ionising = photons, neutrons (n → recoil p = high LET). ≠ "indirect action".

Chromosome aberrations

  • Break fates (3): restitution, deletion, exchange.
  • Before replication (G1/early S) → chromosome-type (both chromatids); after (late S/G2) → chromatid-type.
  • Lethal (3): dicentric, centric ring, anaphase bridge. Non-lethal (mutation): reciprocal/symmetric translocation (stable, oncogene), small deletion (terminal/interstitial), interphase rearrangement†.
  • Acrocentric: 13, 14, 15, 21, 22. Robertsonian 13/21 or 14/21 → carrier healthy, child Down = genetic effect.
  • Dicentric = biodosimetry marker: 2 centromeres, easy to see, specific (background ~1/1000 cells), yield rises with dose, lymphocytes in G0. Translocations (FISH†) for old exposures.
  • Mitotic catastrophe = main death mode after RT; NOT programmed; micronuclei, giant cells.
  • Sensitivity: M ≈ G2 > G1 > early S > late S (most resistant). G2/M "too short to repair"; late S has sister chromatid for HRR.
  • Damage classes (4): non-lethal, sublethal (SLD), potentially lethal (PLD), lethal.

2.1-2.4, 2.7 extras

  • Macroscopic damage sources (3): weapons, disasters, accidents; acute vs chronic. Microscopic (3): DNA damage, aberrations, cell damage. "Lucky draw".
  • Ballistic-gel picture: low-LET sparse (X, γ, e-); high-LET dense (α, C ions, n via recoil p).
  • 4 stages: physical 10-15 s → physico-chemical 10-12 → chemical 10-5-1 s → biological s-years. Chain: IR → excitation (heat) / ionisation → radicals → chemical repair or DNA damage → enzymatic repair or death / mutation (germline → heritable; somatic → cancer).
  • Hereditary myth: radiation makes no new kinds of mutation, only raises frequency → stochastic.
  • BER 4 steps: glycosylase → AP endonuclease → polymerase → ligase. NER: recognise → dual incision → remove oligo → fill/seal.
  • Karyotype at metaphase shows acentric fragment, centric ring, dicentric.

Ch 3 - Effects on humans, history, ARS

History & data sources

  • Fleischman (burns 1897, d.1905), Dally, Geyser (Tricho hair-removal X-ray ~1924). Radithor: Eben Byers ~1400 bottles 1927-30 → radiation hormesis belief.
  • Ra-223 Xofigo (FDA 2013): mCRPC bone mets; t½ 11.43 d; 95.3% energy as α, 1.1% photons (81-84 keV); <0.1 mm range; faeces. fraction = (½)t/t½ (28 d → 18%; 30 d → 16.2%).
  • Mega Mouse (Russells, Oak Ridge, ~7 million mice, 7 loci): conclusions (4) = mutation rate ∝ DNA amount/nucleus size; loci differ ~35x; males more sensitive; time gap before conception reduces effect; + dose-rate/fractionation matter. NOT: "aged mice more sensitive". Selby-Russell dispute → LNT evidence.
  • Human data: dial painters (jaw, bone sarcoma), miners (radon, lung), X-ray pioneers, A-bomb (Hiroshima 6 Aug 1945 U-235; Nagasaki 9 Aug Pu-239; ~120,000 followed, 60,000 cancer deaths by 1990 but only ~5% attributable). Doubling dose ~1 Gy†; children of survivors: no measurable excess.
  • INES: TMI 1979 = 5; Chernobyl 1986 = 7; Tokaimura 1999 = 5 on slide (4 official); Fukushima 2011 = 7.

Fatal cancer ~5%/Sv†; worker limit 20 mSv/y (max 50)†; cataract threshold 0.5 Gy†. Stochastic curve peaks ~2.5 Gy then dips (cell killing). Radiation events are probabilistic.

Dosimetry & biodosimetry

  • TLD: 0.05 mSv - 10 Sv; not for accident victims (no badge).
  • Dicentric assay: blood → PHA† stimulates G0 lymphocytes → colcemid† at metaphase → stain, count. ~500 cells, limit 0.25 Gy, in vivo = in vitro yield. Lymphocytes: long-lived, sensitive, well distributed, G0.
  • Y = c + αD + βD² → D = [-α + √(α² + 4βY)] / 2β. Slide fit α=0.03, β=0.06: Y=0.1 → 1.06 Gy; Y=0.3 → 2.0 Gy. Uncertainty √N (Poisson). Data: 1 Gy 0.109; 2 Gy 0.316; 5 Gy 1.69.
Technique: yield = dicentrics ÷ cells scored, then quadratic formula, take + root.

Skin & ARS

Skin: mild 6-8 Gy (epilation, erythema); moderate 8-20 (2nd-degree burn); severe >20 (desquamation, necrosis). All deterministic. Chronic → ulceration.

ARS phases (3): prodromal → latent → manifest illness. Time-to-vomit: 1-2 Gy ≥2 h (10-50%); 2-4 Gy 1-2 h; 4-6 Gy <1 h (100%); 6-8 Gy <30 min; >8 Gy <10 min.

Blood-count fall order (= sensitivity): lymphocytes > granulocytes > platelets > erythrocytes. Lymphocytes die by interphase apoptosis (nadir day ~6); granulocytes nadir d 8-10 (live ~1 d†); platelets d 16-18 (8-10 d†); RBC d 18-20 (120 d†). Normal lymph 1-5 G/L, gran 3-7 G/L.

3.3 genetic effects extras

  • Germ cells by meiosis; synapsis (pairing) → crossover; radiation → mis-alignment.
  • Mutations not always bad: Tsien mutated GFP into colours (2008 Nobel).
  • Human: no measurable excess in A-bomb children; wait ~6 months before conceiving†.

4.1-4.3, 4.8 extras

  • Howard & Pelc 1953 named phases (³²P, Vicia faba, autoradiography). Typical: G1 1 h-days (variable), S 6-8 h, G2 3-4 h, M 1 h. Jejunum crypt ~9-10 h; skin basal >200 h.
  • Checkpoints (4): G1/S (p53, p21, Rb, E2F); intra-S (ATM, ATR, CHK1/2); G2/M (CHK1, Cdc25, CDK1-cyclin B); spindle. G2/M arrest: ATM/ATR → CHK1/2 ⊥ Cdc25 → CDK1 stays Tyr-phosphorylated; p53-independent (half of cancers lack p53 → rely on G2/M).
  • PLM damping: variable cycle (mostly G1), uneven label. Modern: BrdU + antibody; flow cytometry (propidium iodide; 2N = G1, 4N = G2/M, between = S).
  • CDK control (5): cyclin binds (on); CAK Thr-P (on, "thrive"); Wee1 Tyr-P (off, "tired"); Cdc25 removes (on); CKIs p21/p27/p16 (off). Cyclin destroyed by ubiquitin-proteasome. CDK1 = Cdc2.
  • Late S resistant: sister template for HR + more thiols†. G2/M sensitive: no time, condensed chromatin, acentric fragments lost. Age response also in crypts; OER ~same across phases; high-LET flattens phase differences.
  • DNA vs RNA: deoxyribose/ribose (2'-OH), T/U, ds/ss. Purines A,G (2 rings); pyrimidines C,T,U. Phosphodiester 3'→5'; antiparallel; 5' end phosphate, 3' end -OH.
  • RNA types: mRNA, rRNA, tRNA (anticodon). Exons/introns, splicing; reverse transcriptase → cDNA (no introns). Genomic vs cDNA library. Restriction site 4-8 nt, palindromic; name = genus+species+strain+number.
  • Gene therapy routes: viral (lenti, retro, adeno, AAV), nanoparticles, gene gun, in vivo electroporation, transposon†, S/MAR†.

Ch 5 - Survival curves (heaviest chapter)

Clonogenic assay

Survival = can form colony ≥ 50 cells (5-6 divisions), 1-2 weeks; gold standard; cancer cells ignore contact inhibition. Log SF vs linear dose.

PE = colonies / seeded (0 Gy)    SF = colonies / (seeded x PE)

80/100 → PE 0.8; 120/(1000x0.8) = 0.15. Seed more cells at high dose to get countable colonies.

Single-target single-hit (STSH)

SF = e-D/D₀

Poisson: P(0 hits) = e-m. D₀ = mean 1 hit/cell = 37% dose. Straight line, no shoulder (Dq = 0). Lower D₀ = steeper = more sensitive.

D₁₀ = D₀ ln10 = 2.3 D₀; 3.7% at 3.3 D₀; 108 cells x 2 D₀ → 1.35x107. Memorise ln2 = 0.693, ln10 = 2.303, e-1 = 0.37.

Multi-target (MTSH) & two-component

SF = 1 - (1 - e-D/D₀)n    Dq = D₀ ln n    high dose: SF ≈ n e-D/D₀

n = extrapolation number (back-extrapolate straight part to SF axis); D₀ = final slope; Dq = shoulder width (quasi-threshold). Two-component: SF = e-D/D₁[1-(1-e-D/D₀)n], D₁ = initial slope.

n=4, D₀=1.5: Dq = 1.5 ln4 = 2.08 Gy; SF(6 Gy) = 1-(1-e-4)4 = 0.0713; SF(1 Gy)=0.944.
Dq: belongs to MTSH (not STSH), not a DNA-damage threshold, not no. of targets (that is n), fixed property of the curve; hypoxia multiplies D₀ and Dq by OER, n unchanged. Wider shoulder / larger Dq, n, D₁ = more repair = more resistant.

Linear-quadratic (LQ)

SF = e-(αD + βD²)

α (Gy-1) single-track; β (Gy-2) two-track. α/β unit = Gy = dose where αD = βD². Late tissue (cord, lung, kidney) 2-4 Gy (~3); early tissue & tumours ~10 Gy; prostate 2-3 (low). Smaller α/β = wider shoulder = more curved = more fraction-size sensitive.

α=0.3, β=0.03: SF(2) = e-0.72 = 0.487; α/β = 10.

LQ vs MTSH: both are mathematical fits, not biological; LQ has no terminal slope (bends forever), more low-dose curvature; MTSH has terminal slope; both straight for high-LET.

Finding α/β

Fe plot (Douglas-Fowler): 1/D = α/E + (β/E) d → α/β = intercept ÷ slope; x-intercept at d = -α/β. (0.020/0.0067 = 3 Gy.)

Two isoeffective schedules: α/β = (D₂d₂ - D₁d₁) / (D₁ - D₂). 60 Gy@2 vs 40 Gy@4 → (160-120)/(60-40) = 2 Gy.

TCP / NTCP

TCP = e-N·SF    P = 1 / [1 + (D₅₀/D)k]

N clonogens; Poisson zero survivors. Therapeutic window: TCP ≥ 0.5 & NTCP ≤ 0.05. k = steepness.

N=109, D₀=3, TCP 0.9 → N·SF = -ln0.9 = 0.105 → SF = 1.05x10-10 → D = -3 ln(SF) = 68.9 Gy. 40 Gy, D₀ 2.2 → 12.7 survivors → TCP ~0. Fractionated effective D₀ ~3 Gy; ~7 Gy per decade.

5.1-5.4, 5.7e, 5.11 extras

  • Survival = reproductive integrity; reproductive (mitotic/clonogenic) death; non-dividing cells (nerve, muscle): death = loss of function†.
  • Radiosensitivity (killing) ≠ responsiveness (speed of shrinkage, depends on turnover).
  • Survival depends on (7): dose, LET, dose rate, cycle phase, differentiation, sensitisers/-SH, oxygen.
  • Assay steps (8): monolayer → trypsin → single-cell suspension → count → seed (more cells at higher dose; aim 50-150 colonies†) → irradiate → incubate 1-2 wk 37 °C → fix, stain, count ≥50. Slide: 100/400/1000/10000 seeded → 90/72/36/45 colonies → PE 0.9, SF 0.2/0.04/0.005. Uses: therapeutic ratio, WR, RBE, OER.
  • Semi-log plot: log SF never reaches zero; exponential = straight line. Shapes (2): straight (high-LET, very sensitive cells) vs shoulder (X-rays, SLD repair; wider shoulder = more repair).
  • Slide LQ fit: α 0.091, β 0.074 → α/β 1.23 Gy (broad shoulder, late-like). Animal α/β: skin 9-12, jejunum 6-10, colon 10-11, testis 12-13; cord 1.7-4.9, kidney 1-2.4, lung 2-6.3, bladder 3.1-7.
  • Widen therapeutic window (3): fractionation; dose shaping (IMRT, protons) shifts NTCP right; sensitisers shift TCP left.

Split dose: 0.005 → 0.02 (x4); PLDR 0.01 → 0.04. SFsplit = [SF(D/2)]². 24 °C between doses: repair yes, cycling no → better survival, no dip.

Dose rate

  • Lower rate → less effect (SLDR during exposure); effect range 0.01-1 Gy/min; >1 Gy/min = acute; killing falls clearly below ~30 cGy/h.
  • Broad-shoulder cells show largest dose-rate effect.
  • Inverse dose-rate effect: HeLa 0.37 Gy/h kills more than 1.54 Gy/h (cells pile up at G2 block). Order as rate falls: acute → repair → redistribution → proliferation; α component never removed.
  • LDR brachytherapy Ir-192 (was Ra-226): 50-70 Gy over 5-9 d (~0.4 Gy/h = 0.007 Gy/min); effective D₀ ~3 Gy. Intracavitary vs interstitial.

Fractionation maths

SFtotal = (SFd)n   D0,eff = -d / ln SFd   D10,eff = 2.303 D0,eff

SF(2 Gy)=0.5 → D0,eff = 2/0.693 = 2.885 Gy; 30 fx → 0.530 = 9.3x10-10.: D10,eff 2.3 Gy → D0,eff 1 Gy; 0.25x4 = 1 Gy = 1 D₀ → 37%.

History: Strandqvist 1944 log-log slope 0.33 (double time → 1.257x dose); Ellis NSD D = NSD·T0.11·N0.24 (historical).

Gaps: H&N -1.4% control/day; cervix -0.5%/day; breast/prostate none; compensate ~0.6 Gy/day (3 d → 4.2%, 1.8 Gy).

Schedules

  • Conventional 1.8-2 Gy/d, 5/wk, 6-7 wk. Hyperfractionation: ≥2/day, ≥6 h apart, spares late tissue. Hypofractionation: fewer, larger; suits low α/β (prostate). Accelerated: shorter overall time vs repopulation. Radiosurgery: one large dose.
  • EORTC: 72 Gy / 45 fx x 1.6 Gy tid / 5 wk → more acute + consequential late effects.
  • CHART: 36 fx / 12 consecutive days, tid 6 h apart, 50.4-54 Gy; spinal cord concern.
  • ARCON = accelerated + carbogen (95% O₂ / 5% CO₂) + nicotinamide.

Cell death (5 modes)

  • Mitotic catastrophe: main RT death; NOT programmed.
  • Apoptosis: programmed, ATP; cytochrome c, Bax, caspase 3, FasL; Bcl-2 blocks. Caspase-8 extrinsic / -9 intrinsic / -3 executioner.
  • Necrosis: swell, burst, inflammation, not programmed.
  • Senescence: alive, never divides. Autophagy: self-digestion (Ohsumi 2016 Nobel).
  • Telomerase; wrong options: alkaline phosphatase, tyrosine kinase, DNA polymerase, cytochrome c.

6.2d, 6.3, 6.6, 6.10 extras

  • Dose "recovered" by split ≈ Dq; SFsplit/SFsingle = n (0.02/0.005 = 4 → n = 4, Dq = 2.08 Gy). No shoulder → no split-dose recovery (high LET).
  • PLDR conditions (4): PBS, plateau phase, delayed plating, delayed tumour explant (Little 1973†). Slide: small tumour 15 Gy rel. survival 1.6 at 6-8 h; large (>1 cm³) 20 Gy 3.8 at 8 h. "Dq: same capacity of SLDR" → slope changes only. Melanoma link (controversial).
  • Fractionation sparing example: D₀ 1.5, n 4: SF(2 Gy) 0.706 → D0,eff 5.7 Gy; 30x2 Gy 3x10-5 vs single 60 Gy 2x10-17. 109 clonogens, 60 Gy, D0,eff 3 → TCP 0.13; D0,eff 2.5 → 0.96.
  • Isoeffect history: Paris ram testes (Regaud†, 1920s) → fractionation sterilises, spares skin; Strandqvist; Ellis NSD (unit ret†; fails for late/large fractions). Isoeffect curve: late tissues steep ("intensive repair"); dog heart 50% effusion: 17 Gy single, 46 @4, 59 @3, 72 @2.
  • Apoptosis pathways (2): extrinsic (FasL → caspase-8) and intrinsic (Bax → cytochrome c → caspase-9) → caspase-3; PS exposed; blebs, apoptotic bodies; secondary necrosis if not phagocytosed. High dose → necrosis; lower → apoptosis. Senescence = short telomeres; autophagosome + lysosome.

RBE = D250 kV / Dtest (same effect)   RBEmax = αtest/αX

Rises slowly <10, steeply 10-100, peak ~100 keV/μm, falls above (overkill). RBE larger at: low dose/fraction (X-ray shoulder), fractionation, low dose rate; depends on endpoint, tissue, O₂. Slide: RBE 3.0 at SF 0.6 → 1.5 at SF 0.01; fractionated 2.6. Slide 10 at 1%: α 280 rad, n 470, X 950 → RBE 3.4 / 2.

Technique: X-ray LQ e-(0.15D+0.05D²), neutron e-0.842D. Set -lnSF; Dn = -lnSF/0.842; DX = [-0.15+√(0.0225+0.2(-lnSF))]/0.1; RBE = DX/Dn: 3.3 (SF 0.6), 2.0 (0.1); max 0.842/0.15 = 5.6.

WR & equivalent dose

H = Σ WR D (Sv)   1 Gy = 1 J/kg

Photons, electrons, muons 1; protons, pions 2; α, fission fragments, heavy ions 20; neutrons energy-dependent: thermal ~2.5, peak ~20.7 at 1 MeV, 8.81 at 10 MeV, 5.5 at 50 MeV. ICRP 103 (2007)†.

2 mGy γ + 0.5 mGy 1-MeV n + 0.1 mGy α = 2 + 10.35 + 2 = 14.35 mSv (neutrons 19% of Gy, 72% of Sv). 3 mGy γ + 1 mGy 10-MeV n = 11.81 mSv.

OER & oxygen fixation

OER = Dhypoxic / Doxic (same effect) ≥ 1

X/γ 2.5-3 (slightly lower ~2 at <2 Gy†); 15 MeV n 1.6; α 1.0. OER falls as LET rises; ~1 at 100 keV/μm. Ranking OER: 1 MV X > 10 MeV p > 3 MeV α; 8.3 MeV α has lower LET, higher OER than 4 MeV α.

Fixation: R• (lives 10-5 s) + O₂ → RO₂• (permanent) vs -SH (GSH → GSSG) restores. O₂ must be present at/within ms of irradiation. Hypoxia: D₀ x OER, Dq x OER, n same (oxic D₀ 1.2, hypoxic 3.0 → OER 2.5 = ratio of D₀ = ratio of Dq).

S(p) = (mp + K)/(p + K), m = 3, K = 3 mmHg; mmHg ≈ %O₂ x 7.6

Air 21% = 155-160 mmHg, S = 2.96; half effect at ~0.5% / 3 mmHg; no change 5%-100%; venous 40 mmHg. Dose factor = 2.96/S: 0.1% 2.11; 0.4% (3 mmHg) 1.48; 1% 1.22; 2% 1.11 (S 2.67 = 90%); 40 mmHg 1.04. 70 Gy at 3 mmHg → 104 Gy.

Tumour hypoxia

  • Thomlinson & Gray 1955: O₂ diffuses ~150 μm; cords <160 μm no necrosis; >200 μm always necrotic; living rim 100-180 μm.
  • Hypoxic fraction 10-20% (~15%), up to 50%. Chronic = diffusion-limited (carbogen, HBOT); acute = perfusion-limited, vessels close (Brown 1979; nicotinamide).
  • HIF-1α (VHL degrades in normoxia; 2019 Nobel Kaelin/Ratcliffe/Semenza) → VEGF, GLUT1, angiogenic switch; CAIX, LOX, MMPs; hypoxia → G1 arrest.
  • Reoxygenation: 14% anoxic → ~100% after 10 Gy → 32% at 1 h → ~20% by 6-12 h. Survivors dominated by hypoxic cells (15% hypoxic → 85% of survivors).
  • Detect: Eppendorf pO₂ electrode† (invasive, points); biopsy CD31 (vessels), HIF-1α, pimonidazole (<10 mmHg); PET: FMISO (2-4 h, low contrast), FAZA, HX4 (faster clearance), Cu-ATSM (not nitroimidazole); FDG ≠ hypoxia. DCE-MRI, BOLD-MRI. Cut-offs TMR > 1.5, TBR > 1.3. Nitroimidazole: 1e- → radical anion; O₂ reverses (futile cycle); no O₂ → R-NHOH trapped.
  • Overcome (4 groups): more O₂ (quit smoking - CO binds Hb 200x, HBOT ~6.6% gain + fire/vasoconstriction, carbogen, nicotinamide, ARCON); O₂ mimic (misonidazole, nimorazole - DAHANCA 5); kill hypoxic (tirapazamine - TROG 98.02); change radiation (high-LET, boost). Hyperthermia. Glutaraldehyde = fixative, NOT sensitiser.
  • HIGRT / dose painting: DPBC (contour) vs DPBN (voxel). Thorwarth 2007: +20% boost → TCP +14.3%. Chang 2013: TCP 73% std, 94% uniform (parotid NTCP +22%), 93% hypoxia-directed (flat).

Tissue sensitivity

  • Bergonie-Tribondeau 1906 (3): mitotically active, high potency / long dividing future, undifferentiated. Vogin 2013 "first approximation". Also suggested carcinogenesis.
  • Rank (slide 50): embryonic > lymphocytes > erythrocytes(precursors) > sperm > epithelial > endothelial > connective > bone > nerve > brain > muscle. Lymphocyte = exception (interphase apoptosis). Sensitive curve = steeper = smaller D₀.
  • Embryo: pre-implantation (human d 1-9) all-or-none death ~80%; organogenesis (d 9-41) malformations ~100%, neonatal death 70%; fetus resistant. A-bomb severe mental retardation: window 8-15 wk, >0.5 Gy (0.5-0.99 Gy 25%; ≥1 Gy 75%; 16-25 wk 39%; >26 wk none).
  • Testis: 0.15 Gy oligospermia; 0.5 Gy azoospermia (temp); 2 Gy ~1 y recovery; permanent 6 Gy single or 2.5-3 Gy fractionated (fractionation worsens!); Leydig resistant → hormones unaffected (17/24 Gy scrotum failed to castrate). Table: 10-30 cGy oligo; 30-50 azoo 4-12 mo; 50-100 all azoo 3-17 mo; 100-200 2-9+ mo; 200-300 may be permanent; 1200/2400 permanent.
  • Ovary: no latent, age-dependent: 12 Gy pre-puberty → 2 Gy near menopause; menopause-like. RT-OA (Asiri 2016): 15 Gy/4-5, 16/4, 20/10; non-invasive, adjustable (RT-OS), 6-8 wk, MRI.

7.6a, 7.8d-e, 7.9 extras

  • Words: normoxia (~21%), hypoxia (low), anoxia (none). Hypoxic cells up to ~3x more resistant.
  • Group 4 change radiation: high-LET beams (OER → 1), fractionation (reoxygenation), boost to hypoxic region.
  • HIGRT 4 steps: hypoxia imaging (PET/MRI) → quantification/segmentation → dose prescription + IMRT plan → evaluation (TCP/NTCP). Slide plan: standard 66.5/57/50/40 Gy; HIGRT adds 76/74/72 Gy.
  • Preclinical: Christian - PET (2.7 mm) vs autoradiography (0.1 mm) volumes matched only 39%; Schutze - FaDu, 25 vs 35 Gy, FMISO-hypoxic tumours worse control, extra dose helped.
  • Planning studies 2001-2015 (13, nearly all HNSCC; Even 2015 NSCLC): Chao 2001 Cu-ATSM TMR>2, 80 Gy; Thorwarth 2007 DPBN; Hendrickson 2011 TCP +17%; Toma-Dasu 2012 121 Gy; Henriques 2014 TCP +18.1%, parotid +4.6%; Even 2015 HX4 129 Gy. 7 trials registered.
  • HIGRT challenges (4): PET resolution; hypoxia changes (acute cycling, reoxygenation); normal-tissue tolerance; full compensation needs 100-150 Gy (boosts limited to ~80-90 Gy).
