Anatomy Label Drill

Recall: click a box, type its name, Enter. Rapid: click a box to reveal, then 1 knew it / 2 didn't. Match: pick a term from the bank, click its box. Assess: 3 random blanks, every other label shown; type each, then ↻ Retry same 3 as often as you like until all three are right. Anything you miss goes on the weak list. Revise: every label is shown for reading; tap Hide labels to blank them all and recall, tap again to show (nothing is scored). Where the same name appears more than once on a slide, one answer fills in every copy. Timed: one flashing box at a time against a clock; beaten in time turns blue, missed or timed out turns red, and every red goes on the red list under the diagram.

The boxes come from text detection on the slide figures, so a few may sit on a caption rather than a true label, and a couple of true labels may be unboxed.
